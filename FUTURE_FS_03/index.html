Before you visit

Do I need an appointment?

Walk-ins are welcome, but a request helps us keep your waiting time short.

What should I bring?

Bring a photo ID, any old prescriptions or reports, and a list of medicines you take.

How long until you confirm my request?

Our reception calls within 2 hours during clinic hours. Requests made after closing are answered the next morning.

Can I change or cancel my slot?

Yes. Call the clinic at least 2 hours before your time and we will move or cancel it.

Which payments do you accept?

Cash, UPI and cards are all accepted at the front desk.
